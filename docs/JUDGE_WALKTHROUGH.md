## 💡 Quick Reference: Answers to Common Judge Questions

- **Q: How are public requirements ingested?**
  - *A: The Ingestion Engine connects to public procurement feeds, RFP registers, and verified executive social posts, parsing raw text into structured requirements with citations.*
- **Q: Does this work without an active internet connection or paid API key?**
  - *A: Yes! IntentOS includes deterministic local heuristics, mock providers, and local WebAudio synthesis, ensuring 100% reliability for offline evaluation.*
- **Q: How does the AI score intent?**
  - *A: It uses a 5-factor weighted algorithm (Requirement Fit 35%, Urgency 25%, Authority 15%, Firmographics 15%, Channel Reliability 10%) providing full transparency.*
